Hi, I’m Seratul.

I’m a paralegal at Khalique Law PLLC, an immigration law firm. I help prepare forms and supporting documents for family petitions, adjustment of status, VAWA cases, and business visas. I also research the laws, regulations, and policy guidance that inform those filings.

My degree is in computer science, and that background still shapes how I work. At the firm, I built a Notion workspace from scratch to organize client information, cases, deadlines, guides, and blog content. I’ve also introduced AI tools into routine drafting work, with rules for protecting client confidentiality and reviewing what the tools produce.

Before joining the firm, I worked as a pharmacy technician at CVS. Filling prescriptions and resolving insurance issues taught me to pay attention to details while keeping the person waiting for an answer in mind.

Where the technical side started

At ROSP, I led development of a Flutter app and web portal for invoicing. Before that, I managed data at Khan’s Tutorial, connecting QuickBooks with Google Sheets, writing scripts for daily records, and helping move classes online.

I enjoy learning how a new field works and finding ways to make its everyday tasks clearer and easier to manage. This site brings together that experience and some of the projects behind it.
